# Ascend — Product Plan v2: Teach · Diagnose · Prepare · Win the Parent

*Planning doc — June 2026. Goal: make Ascend the product parents actively switch TO.*

## North star

A parent switches products when three sentences become true:
1. **"It actually teaches my kid"** (not just drills numbers at them)
2. **"It tells me exactly what's wrong and hands me the fix"** (not just a percent score)
3. **"It gets my kid ready for the FAST test"** (the outcome Florida parents are judged on)

Everything below serves one of those three.
